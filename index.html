Problem Statement Context

Convective weather hazards such as severe thunderstorms, hailstorms, cloudbursts, and lightning develop rapidly within localized spatial scales and short timeframes. Traditional regional numerical weather prediction (NWP) models often fail to capture sub-hour cell growth.

Our AI Solution

Alertora AI introduces a multi-modal convective nowcasting system that fuses high-frequency Doppler Weather Radar (DWR) reflectivity, INSAT-3D multispectral satellite imagery, ground lightning networks, and AWS ground telemetry to produce accurate 0–6 hour hyper-local hazard forecasts.

Key Stakeholders & Beneficiaries

  • State Disaster Management Authorities (SDMA / EOC)
  • Indian Meteorological Department (IMD) & Regional Met Centers
  • Emergency Response Units & Municipal Dewatering Teams
  • Farmers, Schools, Ports & General Public Citizens
